# Hi, I'm Ian Welkins

I build free online calculators — small, single-purpose tools that do one job well. Every calculator I publish shows its working: the formula or chart behind it, the published source it comes from, and a worked example. No sign-up, no accounts, nothing to install.

## My calculator sites

- [Candle Wax Calculator](https://candlewaxcalc.info) — how much wax, fragrance and dye a candle needs
- [Cross Stitch Calculator](https://crossstitchcalc.online) — fabric size, thread and skein estimators for stitchers
- [Gravel Calculators](https://gravelcalculators.online) — gravel, sand and aggregate volumes for drives and paths
- [Retirement Date Calculator](https://retirementdatecalc.online) — retirement dates and countdowns by country and scheme

More tools are in the works. If a calculator gets something wrong, every site has a report-an-error page — reports are read and checked against the published sources.
